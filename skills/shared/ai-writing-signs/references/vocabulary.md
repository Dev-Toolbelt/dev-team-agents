# AI Vocabulary

Words the source page lists as overused by language models, grouped by the period in which they were most frequent. The lists come from English-language studies; the Portuguese column gives the usual equivalent, which is a weaker signal. One word is coincidence. A cluster in one paragraph is the sign.

Use the plain word in the last column.

|---|---|---|

## Not from the source

Frequent in model-written product and marketing copy, added here from practice: *seamless*, *navigate* (a challenge), *ensuring* and *reflecting* as trailing clauses (§ 1 Superficial analysis in the skill). *robust* and *leverage* also appear on the source page. Same rule: the plain word.

The list moves as models change. Treat a new cluster of fashionable abstract words the same way.
